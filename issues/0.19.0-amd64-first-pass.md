Title: Compatibility first pass on AMD64 (0.19.0): Jedi Academy out-of-memory crash, four guest hangs, and display questions

## Setup

A scripted first pass over 77 NTSC-U titles: 45 s per game, judged from screenshots. Gameplay wasn't tested.

- **hakuX:** 0.19.0 release, `hakux-0.19.0-x86_64.tar.gz` (01ded7c8eb), KVM CPU backend, release default core options (`render scale 2x, overscan cropped, screen normal, language 1 (auto), video region auto, rumble off, network off, resampler sinc, audio processing dsp, frame pacing off, communicator off`).
- **Frontend:** RetroArch 1.22.2 on KMS (Vulkan, `khr_display`, 1920x1080), PulseAudio. Input came from RetroArch's network RetroPad on player 1.
- **Machine:** CachyOS, Linux 7.3.1; Ryzen 7 9800X3D; Radeon RX 9070 XT, RADV, Mesa 26.2.6, Vulkan 1.4.354.
- **Images:** CHDs made with `chdman createdvd` from XISOs rewritten by `extract-xiso -r`.
- **Run per game:**
  - RetroArch is freshly started with an empty profile and a new console disk.
  - `HAKUX_LR_STATS=1 HAKUX_DISP_STATS=1` are set.
  - Screenshots are taken at 10 s, 22 s and 45 s.
  - Start and A are pressed in turn every 2.5 s from 10.5 s to 33 s, then all buttons are released at 35 s.
  - Quit is sent at about 46 s, with a 60 s hard limit.

Results: 72 runs completed, 4 hung and 1 crashed. Five titles reached gameplay and 60 a menu. The rest are below, or were still in intro movies at 45 s.

## Problems

### 1. Star Wars: Jedi Academy: guest out of physical memory, then an unhandled access violation

**Steps:** boot, then press Start and A through to Difficulty and pick Jedi. About 26 s after launch, as the game opens its first movie, the core stops and RetroArch returns to its menu.

```
hakuX-native-io: open \Device\CdRom0\base\video\ja01_e.bik -> 
hakuX-native: vm: out of physical memory for 16 KiB at d0110000 (0 KiB free)
hakuX-native: vm: out of physical memory for 16 KiB at d0050000 (0 KiB free)
hakuX-native: vm: out of physical memory for 16 KiB at d0130000 (0 KiB free)
hakuX-native: seh: t8 access violation at 0029c694, reading 00000000: to the title's handlers
hakuX-native-frontier: stop: seh: unhandled exception c0000005 at 0029c694 (no handler took it), reading 00000000
```

**Expected:** the movie plays and the first level loads.

0.19.0 already includes #69, the fix for Halo's level-load crash, so this is a different case of guest RAM running out. No other title in the run logged `out of physical memory`.

### 2. Guest hangs: guest CPU busy, nothing drawn

Four titles stop drawing for the rest of the run, while the core reports the guest CPU busy (`halted 0%`) and the renderer idle:

| Title | Hang starts | Screen | Sound | Quit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Oddworld: Munch's Oddysee | frame 117, ~6 s after launch | black | none at all | core didn't stop in 10 s, ended the process |
| Project: Snowblind | frame 19, ~2 s | frozen grey gradient (sky over a flat grey plane) | none at all | the same |
| Project Gotham Racing | frame 1386, ~31 s | frozen garage shot from the intro | first ~12 s only | the same |
| Prince of Persia: The Sands of Time | frame 188, ~8 s | Bink Video logo | first ~8 s only | stopped normally |

```
hakuX-hle-hitch: HANG ongoing 43.52 s since frame 19: JIT compiling: 99% of the vCPU in FEX | render idle for 43524 ms | vCPU cpu 43411 ms halted 0% | render cpu 0 run 0 idle 43524 compile 0 (0) submit 0 (1) gpu 0 display 0 | ...
hakuX-native-frontier: stop: the front end stopped the console
hakuX-libretro: core: stopped
hakuX-libretro: vulkan: RetroArch destroyed the context: the core did not stop in 10000 ms (the devices and the renderer): ending the process
```

**Expected:** the intro and the title screen.

In three of the four, Quit falls back to ending the process after 10 s. Each hang was seen once; I haven't retried them or tried them on ARM64.

### 3. Hang reports on KVM show no guest state

Every `hang vCPU` line in the run shows the general registers as zero: 66 samples from 33 titles, including short stalls that recover. `esp` is always one of `d0050ddc`, `d0060ddc` or `d0090ddc`.

```
hakuX-hle-hitch: hang vCPU: eip ... eflags 00000202 (IF 1) irql 0 halted 0 eax 00000000 ebx 00000000 ecx 00000000 edx 00000000 esi 00000000 edi 00000000 ebp 00000000 esp d0050ddc fs 80020000
```

The reason also reads `JIT compiling: …% of the vCPU in FEX`, but this build runs the KVM backend, with no FEX. So on x86-64 the report can't locate a hang, including the four above.

## Worth a look

### 4. Prince of Persia: The Two Thrones and Warrior Within: washed-out menus

- **The Two Thrones:** the title screen and Create New Profile screen are pale green-yellow.
- **Warrior Within:** the Create New Profile screen is mostly white and pink behind the red emblem.

I haven't compared either with real hardware or xemu.

### 5. Far Cry Instincts and Evolution swap over 1,000 times a second

- **Swaps:** `hakuX-hle-fps` counts 1,000–1,600 swaps a second in most 5 s windows, with lows of 52 and 418.
- **Shown:** `hakuX-hle-disp` reports 37–57 new frames a second.

Is this how the titles present (immediate interval), and does the Xbox pace it? It may cost CPU time or game speed; I haven't checked either in gameplay.

### 6. Call of Cthulhu: Dark Corners of the Earth: main menu at 20 fps

Displayed frames per 10 s: 32.5 and 29.5 on the loading screen, then 19.9 and 19.9 in the main menu, a third of the refresh rate. To check against hardware.

### 7. Need a longer run

- **Cold Fear:** after the Ubisoft logo, a black screen with a spinning red radar icon from 22 s to 45 s, displaying 30 frames a second throughout (no hang reported).
- **Splinter Cell: Pandora Tomorrow:** title screen at 10 s, then a 10 s stall from frame 636 (about 11–21 s after launch), and the same still image (a harbour at sunset) at 22 s and 45 s while sound plays.

Not listed: titles still in intro movies at 45 s, and titles where the A presses typed into a name entry. Those are limits of the 45 s plan.
