How each game was tested

Each test uses a fresh RetroArch profile and console disk. Screenshots: boot at 10 s, mid at 45 s. Start/A alternate every 2.5 s from 10.5 s. From 30 s, Claude Haiku checks a frame every 20 s for player-controlled gameplay. Two consecutive gameplay verdicts end the run; the last frame becomes final. Otherwise it ends at 300 s (hard limit: 330 s). Menu stalls may use per-game input overrides. The 0.19.0 tests used a 45 s plan without a judge.

Status is the furthest stage confirmed by review: Boot (started or loading), Intro (logos, movies or legal screens), Menu (title screen, menus or a new game still loading or playing its opening), In-game (gameplay with a crash, freeze, silence, low frame rate or major graphics fault), Playable (gameplay with no observed problem that prevents play). Ratings use screenshots, frame rates, audio measurements and logs from a short run; they do not cover a full playthrough.

Outcome: Completed (plan finished and emulator closed), Crash (emulator or core stopped unexpectedly), Timeout (hard limit or unresponsive emulator), Needs manual input (set during review). A timeout alone does not establish incompatibility; reaching gameplay alone does not establish playability. Results apply only to the listed setup.

Audio is measured from a private sink. Level means the loudest 0.5 s RMS window.
